**Accelerate and simplify AI integration for creators and enterprises with PUMA!**

🌟 **Project Goals:**
- Empower creators and enterprises with cutting-edge AI capabilities.
- Provide a suite of open-source tools for generative content and computer vision.
- Ensure portability, universality, modernity, and affordability.

📅 **Roadmap:**
- **2025**: Launch of the initial suite of tools, including support for Mistrall Small 3, Deepseek R1, and Qwen VL models.
- **Ongoing**: Expansion of supported models and features based on community input and ecosystem growth.

---

## Key Features

### 🌐 **Portability**
- **Multi-Platform Support**: Seamlessly run on consumer hardware with support for Windows, macOS, and Linux.
- **Cloud-Native Compatibility**: Deploy effortlessly on AWS and Azure.

### 🌍 **Universal**
- **Multilingual Documentation**: Comprehensive documentation translated into as many languages as possible, with a special focus on the French community.
- **Accessible Processes**: Detailed documentation of development processes and outputs, making AI accessible to everyone, not just coders.

### 🕒 **Modern**
- **Cutting-Edge Integration**: Stay ahead with support for the latest AI models and maintain high availability.
- **Continuous Improvement**: Regular updates to ensure the suite remains modern and efficient.

### 💸 **Affordable**
- **Forever Free**: PUMA will always be free and open-source, ensuring everyone can benefit from advanced AI technologies.
- **Community-Driven**: Contribute, collaborate, and help shape the future of AI with an active and passionate community.
